Theory of Fusion Plasmas

Joint Varenna-Lausanne International Workshop, since 1988

"Piero Caldirola" International Centre for the Promotion of Science and International School of Plasma Physics

Location: Villa Monastero, Varenna, Italy 2026 Workshop Archive

The Joint Varenna–Lausanne Workshop is a biennial scientific meeting devoted to the theory of magnetic-confinement fusion plasmas, bringing together senior, early career, and student researchers to review and discuss advances in the theory of magnetic confinement systems. Organized and sponsored by EPFL's Swiss Plasma Center, together with the Piero Caldirola International Center for the Promotion of Science and International School of Plasma Physics, the Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca, and the Istituto per la Scienza e la Tecnologia dei Plasmi. The workshop features invited talks and contributed poster presentations, and publishes both in refereed special issues of Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion.

Workshop Focus

Theory of Magnetic Confinement Systems

  • Anomalous Transport (Turbulence, Coherent Structures, Microinstabilities)
  • RF Heating and Current Drive
  • Non Axisymmetric Configurations
  • Macroinstabilities and Magnetic Reconnection
  • Theoretical Aspects of Plasma-Edge Physics and Divertors
  • Theoretical issues related to burning plasmas

Papers and Proceedings

Both invited talks and contributed posters are published in the same special issue of Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion following the conference. Papers are refereed (partially) by participants, a long-standing tradition of the Varenna conference.

The issue will be edited by P. Helander and J. P. Graves, and papers will appear online shortly after acceptance.

Submission details and deadlines for the 2026 special issue are available on the 2026 workshop archive page.
